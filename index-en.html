01 · Executive summary USD terms

Net assets closed the month at USD 123,523, a change of −3.63% from USD 128,177 at 30 Sep 2026. The portfolio remains 55% liquid (USD 67,659) following the exit of the digital-asset position on 24 October. Since inception, measured from the first valuation on 18 Aug 2026 (net assets USD 122,821), the portfolio is +0.57%.

The month was driven by three separate developments, only one of which was a portfolio action: a valuation-led de-rating in KAP, a continued re-rating in Consun, and a rule-based exit from bitcoin ahead of the market disruption around the Binance incident. Commentary on each follows.

KAP plc — beta, not a broken thesis

KAP's decline this month was beta, not a thesis break. Nuclear was the best-performing theme in the period, which makes the underperformance a valuation (duration) event rather than an earnings event. The bear case that spot strength is failing to pass through into contract prices is not yet reflected in the valuation — and it does not survive contact with history: contract realisations have lagged spot by roughly two to four quarters rather than decoupling from it. The second leg of the bear case — that Russia will most likely keep supplying sulfuric acid, easing pressure on Kazakh in-situ leaching — we treat as plausible; its effect is to soften near-term conversion tightness, not to invalidate the multi-year contracting cycle. Position unchanged at 754 shares. The watchline was re-triggered at month-end (the shares had rebounded to USD 65.80 on 28 October before falling back) and was reviewed on 1 November: rating Watch, position held with no addition, and any addition now requires both price confirmation and the 2027 acid contract.

Consun Pharmaceutical — fundamentals improving

Valuation is still repairing: the shares rose from HK$14.84 to HK$15.25 over the month (+2.8%). We trimmed 2,000 shares at HK$16.10 on 21 October, a 2.4% premium to that day's close, on a tactical overbought signal (CLV +0.378, close in the upper part of the range, unfavourable next-day odds) rather than any change in the investment case; net proceeds were HK$32,138.64 after HK$61.36 of fees, and the 5,000-share core position is retained at an all-in cost of HK$19.37. The finerenone threat has been brought forward from 2H2027 to immediate, transmitted through earlier price pressure on Uremic Clearance. CDE review progress and monthly Uremic Clearance sales are now standing agenda items.

Digital assets — the rule exited before the event

The systematic overlay exited the entire digital-asset position on 24 October at US$80,614.63, ahead of the market disruption around the Binance incident; BTC closed the month at US$77,368.77, 4.0% below the exit price. We would not present a single favourable outcome as evidence of skill: the same rule generated five legs — two and a half round trips — and US$2,116.09 of round-trip friction within the month, two of which were whipsaws. The sleeve is now held in USDC yielding 2.75%.

Manager's commentary reflects the manager's judgement at the reporting date and is not a statement of verified fact. Holdings and prices are as reported in the tables above; transaction detail is in Section 04.
